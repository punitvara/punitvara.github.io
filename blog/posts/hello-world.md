I've spent a lot of time writing courses and crash-course guides on this site, but never a proper blog. This is me fixing that. Consider this post the ribbon-cutting.

The plan is simple: short, practical write-ups on the things I'm actually working on — GPU engineering, ML infrastructure, systems programming, and the occasional detour into whatever rabbit hole I fell into that week.

No fixed schedule, no filler. If I write something here, it's because I learned something worth sharing, or got something wrong the first time and want to save you the trouble.

Thanks for stopping by. More soon.
