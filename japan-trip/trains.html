🚄 Leg 1 · 8 April · Shinjuku → Kyoto Station

Door to door ≈ 3 hTrain time ≈ 2h15 (Nozomi)1 transfer≈ ¥14,170–14,370 per adult
  1. Shinjuku → Tokyo Station
    Take the JR Chuo Line Rapid (orange) bound for Tokyo. Platforms 7 or 8 at Shinjuku. Ride ≈ 14 min, no stops to worry about. verify platform
  2. Change at Tokyo Station (allow 15 min)
    The Chuo Line platforms are upstairs, on the opposite side from the bullet trains. Follow the signs to the Tokaido Shinkansen transfer gate. With luggage and a baby, use the elevators.
  3. Tokyo → Kyoto
    Board the Tokaido Shinkansen Nozomi bound for Shin-Osaka (or Hakata). Pick a reserved seat. Ride ≈ 2h15–2h20. Kyoto is the stop after Nagoya. Tokyo platform usually 14–19
  4. Arrive Kyoto Station
    Shinkansen platforms 11–14 (south side, Hachijo). Walk to the main concourse for your hotel exit, or take the Karasuma (north) exit for central Kyoto.
Tip: Nozomi is not covered by the JR Pass. Hikari (~2h40) is a calmer, slightly cheaper option. Book the earliest reasonable train so you arrive at Kyoto by midday.
Luggage: large suitcases may need an oversize-baggage reservation. Alternatively forward bags with takkyubin from your Shinjuku hotel.

✈️ Leg 2 · 13 April · Kyoto Station → Kansai Airport (KIX)

≈ 75–85 minDirect, no changeEvery ~30 min≈ ¥2,850–3,490 per adult
  1. Buy ticket and go to Platform 30
    Take the JR Haruka Limited Express bound for Kansai Airport. Haruka usually leaves from Platform 30 at Kyoto Station (check the board). verify platform
  2. Ride ≈ 75–85 min
    The train runs straight to Kansai Airport, with big luggage racks at the end of each car. Pick a reserved seat for easier travel with a baby.
  3. Arrive Kansai Airport Station (KIX)
    Walk to Terminal 1, check in and drop bags. Allow 2.5–3 h before the flight.
Timing: the first Haruka from Kyoto is about 05:45. The last is around 20:15–20:30. Leave Kyoto at least 4 h before your flight.
Cheaper but not advised: JR Special Rapid + Kansai Airport Rapid via Osaka costs less but needs a change with bags and a baby.

🛒 Where to book

  • Shinkansen (Tokyo → Kyoto): SmartEX is JR Central's official English app and site and accepts overseas cards. Booking usually opens about 1 month before travel verify. You can also buy at a JR ticket office or machine at Tokyo Station.
  • Haruka (Kyoto → KIX): buy at the Kyoto Station JR ticket office or a ticket machine the day before or on the day. No need to pre-book months ahead. Resellers like Japan Experience sell vouchers you swap at the station. Compare the price with the station price first.
  • Chuo Rapid: no booking. Tap an IC card (Suica or ICOCA via Apple or Google Wallet if supported, or a physical card) or buy a ticket at the machine.
  • Official timetables: JR Central · JR West
Booking tip: reserve the Shinkansen seats together, with luggage space (last row or the oversize-baggage seats).

🔎 Check before you travel

  • Official timetables: JR East · JR Central (Shinkansen) · JR West (Haruka)
  • Infant fares on both legs. Children under 6 usually travel free without a seat, but confirm.
  • Platform numbers change. Always confirm on the departure board in the station.
